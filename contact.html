Artwork File Checklist

Ensure optimal press calibration by checking these prepress standards before uploading:

  • Color Space: CMYK (FOGRA51 / GRACoL)
  • Resolution: 300+ DPI at 100% scale
  • Bleed Allowance: 3mm (0.125 in) trim bleed
  • Typography: Fonts outlined to vector paths
  • Safe Zone: Text 4mm within trim line
