Engineering Deep Dive

Uncompromising Color Fidelity at Scale

Offset lithography remains the gold standard for projects demanding flawless color consistency, razor-sharp vector line art, and exceptional unit economy.

Our Heidelberg XL 106 presses employ an indirect offset process: ink is transferred from etched aluminum plates to rubber blankets, then smoothly pressed onto paper. This eliminates toner banding, paper warping, and edge distortion common with digital copiers.
