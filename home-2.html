High-Efficiency Protocol

End-to-End Commercial Offset Workflow

01

Automated CTP Prepress

Spectral PDF preflight and 2400 DPI thermal Computer-to-Plate laser imaging ensure sub-pixel dot positioning and trap alignment.

02

Multi-Color Press Run

Heidelberg Speedmaster multi-unit offset printing at speeds up to 18,000 sheets/hour with closed-loop spectrophotometer ink key controls.

03

Inline Aqueous & Dry

Protective aqueous or UV sealers prevent ink transfer, scuffing, and smudging, allowing instant passage to post-press finishing.

04

Precision Bindery & Freight

Automated folding, saddle-stitching, die-cutting, shrink-wrapping, and palletized distribution directly to regional hubs or fulfillment centers.
