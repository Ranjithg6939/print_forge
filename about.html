Core Pillars

Principles That Guide Every Press Run

Micron Precision

Registration tolerance maintained within 0.05mm across all color units, ensuring razor-sharp typography and image clarity.

Pigment Purity

We blend custom Pantone formulas in our dedicated ink laboratory using pure, heavy-body vegetable and bio-renewable pigments.

Zero Brokerage

Every step—prepress, CTP, lithography, die-cutting, embossing, and binding—is executed inside our secure 75,000 sq. ft. plant.

FSC Stewardship

Committed to protecting our forests through FSC and PEFC chain-of-custody sourcing and zero VOC waterless washouts.

Technical Manufacturing Workflow

The Offset Printing Process

A step-by-step engineering walkthrough of how digital concepts and packaging dielines become tangible, high-precision print products.

STEP 01
Phase 01

Design

First, decide on all the fonts, layout, colors and special finishes that will be used to create the final packaging design.

Vector CAD Dielines
Typography Outlines
PMS & CMYK Colors
STEP 02
Phase 02

Prepress

Digital files are checked to ensure consistent printability and repeatability. The file format is sent to the offset printer CMYK or PMS to communicate more accurate color outputs.

RIP & Trap Proofing
Thermal CTP Plates
Fogra 51 Standards
STEP 03

Types of Offset Printing

Web-Fed Rolls of paper are fed continuously through the press and can produce over 5,000 printed materials.
Sheet-Fed Sheets of paper are individually fed through the press and can produce 12,000 - 24,000 sheets per hour.
Phase 03

Printing

Aluminum plates are used to transfer artworks onto a rubber blanket that is then rolled onto sheets of a chosen material featuring your dieline.

Inpress Color Scans
18k SPH Press Speed
Pure Vegetable Inks
STEP 04
Phase 04

Finishing

Special finishes such as coatings and laminates, are applied and dieline is cut, scored, folded and assembled for delivery.

Soft-Touch Velvet
Hot Foil & Emboss
Precision Die-Cut
STEP 05
Phase 05

Delivery

Most packaging types are shipped flat for convenient assembly upon arrival. Rigid (set-up) boxes will be shipped already assembled.

Flat or Assembled
Corner Guard Shrink
Palletized Freight
