Terms of Service & Prepress Standards
1. File Submission & Preflight

All artwork submitted to PrintForge must be in print-ready PDF/X-4 format with a minimum of 300 DPI resolution at 100% scale. A standard 3mm bleed margin must be included across all exterior trim edges.

2. Color Fidelity & Fogra Standards

Press runs are calibrated to ISO 12647-2 (Fogra 51/52). RGB files will be converted to CMYK using certified color profiles. Critical spot colors must be clearly designated using Pantone solid coated formulas.

3. Production Tolerances

Commercial offset registration and die-cutting tolerances are maintained within ±0.5mm. Final paper stock bulk may vary by ±5% based on mill humidity and manufacturing variance.

4. Proof Approval & Turnaround

Turnaround timelines commence upon formal client sign-off of the digital contract proof or press hard proof. Any subsequent revisions require plate re-imaging and may adjust ship dates.
