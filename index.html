Workflow Precision

Our Industrial Press Workflow

From calibrated prepress proofing to high-speed automated folding and shipping.

01

Prepress & CTP Proofing

Direct-to-plate thermal laser imaging with 2400 DPI optical calibration, RIP verification, and Fogra contract color proofs.

02

Offset Press Run

High-speed Heidelberg 8-color printing with closed-loop spectrophotometer scans maintaining precise ink-key density every 500 sheets.

03

Finishing & Embellishment

Precision die-cutting, soft-touch thermal lamination, spot UV coatings, foil stamping, Smyth sewing, and case binding.

04

Quality QA & Dispatch

100% optical inspection, humidity-controlled shrink wrapping, pallet strapping, and direct global freight distribution.
